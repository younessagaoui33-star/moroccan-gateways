About Us

A local team that designs Morocco around you.

Our story

Moroccan Gateways is a local team with more than 5 years of experience in tourism. We design private tours, transport, guides and activities across Morocco, from the imperial cities to the Sahara, so you can discover its culture, history and landscapes at your own pace.

Who we are

We are a Moroccan team of travel professionals, local guides and drivers who have known these roads and cities all our lives. We know the alleys of Fes as well as the dunes of Merzouga, and we take you to places most visitors miss.

Our services

🧭

Tell us your interests, dates and budget and we build the route around you.

🔒

Private tours

Just your group, a dedicated guide and the freedom to set your own pace.

🏜️

Sahara adventures

Camel treks, 4x4 tours and nights in a desert camp among the Erg Chebbi dunes.

🏛️

Cultural & heritage tours

Medinas, kasbahs and UNESCO sites such as Fes, Volubilis and Ait Benhaddou.

🏍️

Adventure & fun

Quad, buggy and horseback rides, sandboarding and stargazing in the desert.

Why choose Moroccan Gateways?

  • Local expertise: we are a Moroccan team, so you get authentic experiences off the beaten path.
  • Guides in your language: English, Spanish and French.
  • Comfort and safety: our own comfortable tourist car and 4x4, driven by experienced drivers.
  • Personal service: every trip is adapted to your interests, pace and time.
